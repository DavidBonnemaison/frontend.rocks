## TL;DR

`srcset` and `sizes` let the browser pick the smallest sharp image before layout runs. Add `width` and `height` to reserve space against CLS, `fetchpriority="high"` on the hero LCP image, and `decoding="async"` on offscreen images to keep bitmap decompression off the main thread.

## Under the Hood

Raw compressed bytes (AVIF, WebP, JPEG) cannot be painted directly to screen; the engine must decompress them into an uncompressed RGBA bitmap in RAM (a 2000×1500 image expands to 12 MB) and upload it to [GPU Compositor Layers](/concepts/gpu-compositor-layers).

1. The [Preload Scanner](/concepts/preload-scanner-hints) reads `srcset`, `sizes`, and `devicePixelRatio` before CSS finishes parsing, queuing only the best-fitting candidate.
2. `width` and `height` HTML attributes compute an intrinsic `aspect-ratio` box during initial layout, preventing CLS.
3. `decoding="async"` moves bitmap decompression onto a background thread so scrolling never drops a frame.

```html
<picture>
  <source type="image/avif" srcset="/hero-800.avif 800w, /hero-1600.avif 1600w" sizes="100vw" />
  <img src="/hero-800.jpg" width="800" height="450" fetchpriority="high" decoding="sync" alt="Hero" />
</picture>
```

## Problem Genealogy

- **2014 (`<picture>` & `srcset`)**: Ended the era of shipping 4 MB desktop JPEGs to 320 px phones.
- **2019 (`width`/`height` Aspect-Ratio Mapping)**: Browsers mapped HTML dimension attributes to CSS `aspect-ratio`, fixing responsive CLS.
- **2022+ (`fetchpriority` & AVIF)**: Gave developers direct priority control over LCP images without abusing `<link rel="preload">`.

## Production Pitfalls

- **`loading="lazy"` on the hero image**: Lazy-loading above-the-fold images delays the LCP fetch until after layout and intersection checks complete, often adding 500+ ms to LCP.
- **CSS `background-image` for LCP heroes**: Background images stay invisible to the preload scanner until CSS downloads and matches a DOM selector.
- **When not to use `decoding="async"`**: On the primary above-the-fold LCP image, `decoding="sync"` (or omitting `async`) avoids a brief blank frame while the rest of the page paints.
